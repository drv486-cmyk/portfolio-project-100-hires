**Summary:**

**Summary:**
Sangram opens with a few blunt stats: 67% of B2B buyers can now spot unedited AI-generated content, and 58% say it makes them trust a brand less — yet AI-powered outbound has become the norm anyway. Rather than debating whether AI helps or hurts, he pivots to three things he believes haven't changed regardless of AI: GTM is still the actual growth lever (AI is just a tool within it), the CEO has to own GTM or no one will, and a great product with a broken go-to-market still fails. His view is that the teams genuinely winning with AI haven't replaced their fundamentals — they've used AI to automate repetitive work while doubling down on the human judgment parts of GTM, like sequencing and prioritization.

**Key Takeaways:**
- Buyers increasingly notice and distrust generic AI-generated outreach, even as it becomes the default tactic
- AI adoption doesn't replace the need for strong GTM fundamentals and ownership (especially at the CEO level)
- The competitive edge comes from human judgment — deciding what to do and in what order — not from AI tooling itself
- A recurring theme in his content: "AI will make good GTM, better, but it won't make bad GTM, good"
