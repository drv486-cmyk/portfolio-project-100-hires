## Post 1

**Post Date:** Approximately February 2026 ("4mo" relative timestamp at time of research)

**Source:** [https://www.linkedin.com/posts/aadit-palicha_zepto-is-at-the-forefront-of-enterprise-ai-activity-7428837086683869184-eRpY](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/aadit-palicha_zepto-is-at-the-forefront-of-enterprise-ai-activity-7428837086683869184-eRpY)

**Summary:**  
Aadit highlights how Zepto is integrating AI throughout its business operations, positioning the company as an example of enterprise AI deployment in India's consumer technology sector. He describes AI-powered forecasting systems used in supply chain management, automated image analysis in customer support, and advertising products that generate significant revenue for brand partners. He also notes that Zepto's engineering and data science teams use AI-assisted development tools such as Claude Code and Cursor to improve software development efficiency. The post was published alongside Zepto's participation in the India AI Impact Summit, where the company demonstrated both its rapid-delivery infrastructure and AI use cases. Aadit's broader message is that AI adoption is most valuable when embedded deeply into operational systems rather than treated as a standalone initiative.

**Key Takeaways:**

- AI adoption can create value across multiple business functions, including logistics, customer support, software development, and advertising
- Operational AI use cases often generate measurable business outcomes through cost reduction and revenue growth
- Technology leaders increasingly view AI as core infrastructure rather than an experimental side project
- Demonstrating real-world AI implementations can strengthen a company's positioning as an innovation leader

## Post 2

**Post Date:** Approximately December 2025 ("6mo" relative timestamp at time of research)

**Source:** [https://www.linkedin.com/posts/aadit-palicha_over-the-past-12-months-our-operations-and-activity-7402023055356522496-Ppug](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/aadit-palicha_over-the-past-12-months-our-operations-and-activity-7402023055356522496-Ppug)

**Summary:**  
Aadit announces the nationwide rollout of supply-chain automation across Zepto's backend operations. He states that approximately 25 lakh FMCG units are now processed daily through automated systems across the company's network. According to the post, this automation has improved outbound manpower productivity by more than 45% compared with manual operations, generating substantial annual operating cost savings. Aadit emphasizes that the value extends beyond the hardware itself: Zepto's engineering teams have built the software systems that coordinate and integrate these automated assets with the company's logistics infrastructure. He frames this internal capability as a long-term strategic advantage that will enable increasingly sophisticated automation and operational efficiency gains over time.

**Key Takeaways:**

- Operational automation can generate measurable improvements in productivity and cost efficiency when deployed at scale
- Competitive advantage often comes not only from automation hardware but from the proprietary software that orchestrates it
- Investing in internal engineering capabilities creates a foundation for future automation initiatives and operational leverage
- Sharing concrete operational metrics (25 lakh units processed daily, 45%+ productivity improvement) makes company updates more credible and compelling
